Severity: **High** = likely reason for rejection or major revision on its own; **Medium** = likely major-revision point; **Low** = minor.

## Overall assessment

With the final model, the source probe, the per-source table and the run-1/run-2 contrast in place, the manuscript's central finding (internal metrics substantially overstate behaviour on an unseen hospital; class and source are strongly confounded) is better supported than before, but the shortcut evidence is still indirect. Once the seed and ablation runs finish (expected 2026-11-04), items 1 and 5 move from "missing" to "partly addressed" (three seeds; one coarse ablation), which is unlikely to satisfy a strict reviewer on its own. The most likely outcome at BSPC remains **major revision**, mainly on items 4 (no causal shortcut test), 5 (coarse ablation), 6 (one small binary external cohort), 1 (only three seeds) and 14 (one backbone). The strongest parts are the transparency of the data construction, the pre-registered selection and follow-up rules, external reporting with specificity and the exact-class breakdown, and the self-audit. Nothing listed above as running or not done is claimed as done in the manuscript; see the status block at the top of `main.tex`.
