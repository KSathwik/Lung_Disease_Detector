\section{Model and Training}\label{sec:method}
\subsection{Image preprocessing and classifier}
One preprocessing implementation serves training, evaluation and the application. Images are decoded to
three channels, passed through a $3{\times}3$ Gaussian blur ($\sigma{=}0.8$), enhanced with CLAHE
\cite{pizer1987adaptive} on the L channel of CIE LAB (clip limit 2.0, $8{\times}8$ tiles), smoothed with
a Gaussian low-pass filter ($\sigma{=}1.0$), resized to $224{\times}224$ with Lanczos interpolation and
normalised with ImageNet statistics. The feature extractor is DenseNet-121 \cite{huang2017densely} with
ImageNet weights \cite{deng2009imagenet} and frozen BatchNorm statistics. The classification head is
global average pooling, batch normalisation, a dense layer of 256 ReLU units, dropout of 0.3 and a
six-class softmax output.

\subsection{Training procedure}
Training uses Adam \cite{kingma2015adam} in two phases. The head is trained alone for two epochs at a
learning rate of $10^{-3}$; the last 30 backbone layers are then unfrozen and fine-tuned at $10^{-5}$.
The objective is class-weighted cross-entropy, the batch size is 32, and augmentation consists of
horizontal flipping and rotations of up to ten degrees. Early stopping and checkpoint selection use
validation macro-F1, with validation macro ROC-AUC as the tie-breaker. The reported checkpoint for seed
\RSeed{} was selected at epoch \RSelectedEpoch{} of \REpochsTrained{} training epochs; this was the last
epoch, so longer training might change the result.

\subsection{Runs and reproducibility}
The detailed results are for seed \RSeed. The same recipe and partitions were also trained with two
further seeds (seeds \RMSSeeds{} in all) to describe run-to-run variation, and once without the blur, CLAHE
and low-pass filter to examine that design choice. The build used for the final model was chosen on a
common validation set of \RCmpN{} images that were not used for training or testing; internal-test and
external results played no part in the choice. All runs are CPU-trained and time-budgeted.
