\section{Evaluation Protocol}\label{sec:experiments}
The test split and the external cohort are each evaluated once, after the checkpoint is fixed; nothing is
tuned on them. We report accuracy with a 2000-resample bootstrap confidence interval
\cite{efron1994introduction}, macro-F1, per-class precision, recall and F1, one-vs-rest ROC-AUC with
macro average, expected calibration error (ECE, 15 bins), and Wilson intervals \cite{wilson1927probable}
for proportions. Intervals are two-sided at the conventional nominal level of ninety-five percent.

On the external cohort the task is binary. ``Sensitivity'' is the share of TB scans predicted as any class
other than Normal; it is reported only together with specificity, the share of normal scans predicted
Normal. Because any wrong abnormal class meets that definition, we also report the exact Tuberculosis
recall and the ROC-AUC of the probability of ``abnormal'' (one minus the Normal probability). Everything is
computed from saved per-image predictions.
