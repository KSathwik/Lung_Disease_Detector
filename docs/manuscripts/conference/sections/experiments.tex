\section{Evaluation Protocol}\label{sec:experiments}
The internal test split and the external cohort are each evaluated once, after the checkpoint is fixed.
Internal performance is reported as accuracy with a Wilson 95\% confidence interval
\cite{wilson1927probable}, macro-F1, per-class precision, recall and F1, one-vs-rest ROC-AUC with its
macro average, and expected calibration error (ECE, 15 bins). Wilson intervals are used for all reported
proportions; no interval is given for macro-F1.

The external cohort contains only Normal and Tuberculosis labels, so it is not a six-class test.
Sensitivity is the proportion of TB scans predicted as any class other than Normal, and specificity is
the proportion of normal scans predicted as Normal. Because any non-Normal prediction counts as an
abnormal flag under this definition, exact Tuberculosis-class recall is reported separately, together
with the ROC-AUC of the abnormality score (one minus the predicted Normal probability). All metrics are
computed from saved per-image predictions. The external cohort is small and its estimates are not
clinical validation.
