\section{Related Work}\label{sec:related}
Large CXR collections include NIH ChestX-ray14 \cite{wang2017chestxray8}; smaller sets cover
tuberculosis \cite{jaeger2014two,liu2020rethinking}, paediatric pneumonia \cite{kermany2018identifying}
and COVID-19 \cite{cohen2020covid,chowdhury2020can}. DenseNet \cite{huang2017densely} is a common CXR
backbone, for example in CheXNet \cite{rajpurkar2017chexnet}. Cross-hospital performance drops
\cite{zech2018variable}, shortcut learning \cite{geirhos2020shortcut,degrave2021ai}, classification
from image regions without lung tissue \cite{maguolo2020critic}, and label quality
\cite{oakdenrayner2020exploring} motivate our design. We add no new method and do not compare our
figures with those of other papers, because test sets differ.
