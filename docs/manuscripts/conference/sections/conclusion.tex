\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
A leakage-controlled, patient-grouped six-class rebuild (identity-based separation holds only where patient identity is known) gives \RTestAcc\% internal test accuracy with a
standard DenseNet-121, but on an unseen hospital only \RExtSpecK/\RExtSpecN{} normal scans are called Normal
and \RExtTBK/\RExtTBN{} TB scans are called Tuberculosis. Changing the sources moved the external errors to
other classes rather than closing the external gap; this is consistent with, but does not prove, a source shortcut. The work does not show clinical utility or superiority over other
methods. Future work: patient-identified COVID-19 and TB sources and a second external cohort. The model is a
research prototype and must not be used clinically.
