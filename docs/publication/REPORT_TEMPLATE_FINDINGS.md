# M.Tech project report: template findings and proposed standard

Prepared 2026-11-03. Access date for every URL: 2026-11-03.

## 1. Kakatiya University (KU) and KUCE&T: result

**No official KU or KUCE&T M.Tech project report/thesis format was found.** What was checked:
- https://www.kakatiya.ac.in home page, "Courses & Syllabus" (https://kakatiya.ac.in/courses) and the Engineering syllabus page (https://kakatiya.ac.in/syllabus_engineering). It lists B.Tech rules (https://kakatiya.ac.in/uploads/BTECH_RULES_AND_REGULATIONS.pdf, not read), B.Tech and Pre-PhD syllabi and six M.Tech syllabi dated about 2013 (PE, Civil, DC, DE, SE, VLSI/ES). No report-format document is linked. The PG page https://kakatiya.ac.in/pg_courses_syllabus was loaded but its links were not inspected.
- https://kucet.ac.in could not be opened: TLS certificate error (untrusted issuer). It was not bypassed, so KUCE&T site content is unchecked.
- One KU-linked document was read: M.Tech CSE (Software Engineering) regulations and scheme of Kakatiya Institute of Technology & Science, "approved by Board of Studies, CSE, Kakatiya University", effective 2007-2008 (https://kakatiya.ac.in/web/course/226_MTech.%20SE%20Syllabus.pdf). It gives dissertation process rules only: Department Post-Graduate Review Committee; registration, progress and synopsis seminars; report not to be submitted before 48 weeks or after 52 weeks from the start of semester III; viva with one internal and one external examiner. It states no margins, fonts, spacing, chapter order, certificate wording or binding. It is 19 years old and from a different college, so it is not the current KUCE&T rule.
Update 2026-11-03: the student confirmed with the department that there is no prescribed KUCE&T report format and no requirement to publish a paper before dissertation submission. The standard in section 3 is therefore adopted for the report.

## 2. Comparison documents found

### 2.1 Osmania University (University College of Engineering), M.E./M.Tech Rules and Regulations 2022-23
URL: https://uceou.edu/pdfs/circulars/M.E.-M.Tech.Rules%20and%20Regulations%202022-2023%20(Final)%2014-12-2022.pdf (section 9 and Annexure-I; text extracted locally).
- Paper: "A-4 paper approximately 11 Inches x 9 Inches or 27.9 cm x 22 cm", duly hard bound (the stated sizes contradict A4; treat as unclear).
- Body: Times New Roman 12 pt, 1.5 spacing; chapter heading 16 pt, sub-heading 14 pt; left or full justification allowed.
- Margins: left 30 to 40 mm; right, top and bottom 25 to 30 mm (the wording about the right margin is garbled in the source).
- Content: lists of figures, tables, symbols, acronyms before chapter 1; abstract at most 500 words; declaration of originality; total pages normally not above 150.
- Page numbers: pages before chapter 1 in small Roman numerals; text numbering starts at the first page of chapter 1.
- Numbering: headings numbered chapter-wise; equations (Eq. 3.1); tables (Table 3.1, caption at top); figures (Fig 3.1, caption at bottom).
- References: numbered in square brackets in order of appearance, listed after the last chapter, all cited; order author(s), title, publication, year, volume, number, pages. Appendices lettered or Roman, after the last chapter.
- Certificate (Annexure-I): "This is to certify that the Dissertation work entitled <title> submitted by <name (Roll No.)>, a student of Department of <...>, <College> in partial fulfillment of the requirements for the award of the degree of Master of <Engineering/Technology> with <specialization> as specialization is a record of the bonafide work carried out by <him/her> during the academic year <year>", with date and signature lines for supervisor and Head of Department, and a seal. Declaration page: the work is a record of work done by the student; no part copied; data not copied.
- Submission: five copies (clause 5.12); similarity index below 25% before submission (clause 6.16).

### 2.2 JNTUH
No JNTUH-issued M.Tech report format was found.
- R22 M.Tech academic regulations as hosted by a JNTUH constituent college: https://jntuhceh.ac.in/downloads/R22_Academic_Regulations_M.Tech.pdf (text extracted). Dissertation clauses: plagiarism report included in the dissertation; accepted if similarity index is below 30%; at most two resubmissions; three copies certified by the supervisor to the department; publication of a research paper in a UGC-approved journal is listed among requirements and a copy is attached. No margins or fonts.
- IARE (a JNTUH-affiliated autonomous college, not JNTUH): https://iare.ac.in/sites/default/files/downloads/Guidelines_for_M.Tech_Project_Work_Report_Preparation.pdf . A4; Times New Roman 12 pt, 1.5 spacing, fully justified; table and figure text 11 pt; binding-edge margin 3.75 cm, other sides 2.5 cm; 40 to 70 pages from chapter 1; Roman numerals from acknowledgements to chapter 1, then Arabic, bottom centre; chapter heading 18 pt bold centred, section 16 pt, subsection 14 pt bold left; softbound, white cover with department-coloured lettering (CSE navy blue); Turnitin, similarity at most 10%; IEEE or APA reference style; two hard copies plus soft copy.
- Generic search results from other institutes (not authoritative) vary: for example 1 in on all sides, left 1.5 in, or 3.25 cm on the binding side. Not used.

## 3. Proposed formatting standard (to be confirmed with KUCE&T)

| Element | Choice | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Paper | A4 | OU (A4 stated), IARE |
| Margins | Left (binding) 35 mm; right, top, bottom 25 mm | OU left 30 to 40 mm, others 25 to 30 mm; IARE binding 3.75 cm, others 2.5 cm |
| Body font | Times New Roman 12 pt, justified | OU 9.2; IARE |
| Line spacing | 1.5 for text; single for captions, tables, references, block quotes | OU 9.2; IARE (single for tables/captions is the agent's choice) |
| Headings | Chapter title 16 pt bold; section 14 pt bold; subsection 12 pt bold; numbered 1, 1.1, 1.1.1 | OU (16 and 14 pt); IARE (sub-levels) |
| Table and figure text | 10 to 12 pt, never above body size | OU 9.6 and 9.7; IARE |
| Page numbering | Small Roman from the title/certificate pages through the lists; Arabic from chapter 1, bottom centre | OU 9.1 items 12 and 13; IARE |
| Preliminary order | Title page, certificate, declaration, acknowledgements, abstract (max 500 words), contents, list of figures, list of tables, list of abbreviations | OU 9.1 and Annexure-I; exact order is the agent's choice |
| Chapters | Introduction first, literature review in chapter 1 or 2, conclusion and future work last, then references, then appendices | OU 9.1; IARE section 3 |
| Captions | Tables: caption above, "Table 3.1"; figures: caption below, "Fig. 3.1"; equations "(3.1)" | OU 9.5 to 9.7; matches WRITING_BRIEF |
| References | IEEE numbered style, numbers in square brackets in order of first citation, after the last chapter | OU 9.3; IARE 3.7 |
| Length | Normally at most 150 pages (OU); 40 to 70 pages main text is the IARE norm | OU 9.1; IARE |
| Similarity check | Obtain a report. Limits seen elsewhere: 10% (IARE), 25% (OU), 30% (JNTUH R22). KU limit unknown | sources above |
| Binding and copies | OU: hard bound, five copies. KU unknown; ask the department | OU 5.12 and 9 |
| Certificate | Use OU Annexure-I wording as a model with KU, KUCE&T, M.Tech CSE and the academic year filled in; blank signature, date and seal lines only, nothing implied as signed | OU Annexure-I |

If KUCE&T issues its own template it overrides this table. Figures and tables made under the Writing Brief (tables above, figures below) already comply.
