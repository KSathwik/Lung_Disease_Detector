### 1.1 IEEE EMBC 2027 (recommended)
- Official site: https://embc.embs.org/2027/ . 49th Annual International Conference of the IEEE Engineering in Medicine and Biology Society, Suntec Singapore, 11 to 15 July 2027.
- Sponsor: IEEE EMBS (the flagship conference of the society, per the site). IEEE conference record number: not found on the pages read.
- Tracks include Biomedical Imaging and Image Processing, Biomedical and Health Informatics, Therapeutic and Diagnostic Systems (https://embc.embs.org/2027/papers/). AI and data science are named in the theme.
- Deadlines (https://embc.embs.org/2027/important-dates/): full contributed papers submission 24 January 2027; accept/reject 16 April 2027; final submission 30 April 2027. 1-page poster papers: submission 23 April 2027. Portal opens December 2026. All deadlines 11:59 PM AoE, "final and will not be extended".
- Format: 5 pages in total; up to 4 pages technical content including figures and tables, 5th page references only (max 20 references). IEEE template from the paper management system (link on the CFP page). Up to 35 authors. English, original unpublished work.
- Proceedings: the CFP says "1 Page Papers are not eligible for publication and indexing opportunities in IEEE Xplore and PubMed Medline", which implies full papers are. A direct statement that full papers appear in Xplore was not seen. Status: strongly implied, not explicitly confirmed.
- Review: every paper must name a qualified author who agrees to review for EMBC 2027 (reciprocal review, https://embc.embs.org/2027/author-guidelines/). Non-compliant submissions may be desk-rejected. AI use must be disclosed per IEEE policy; unacknowledged generative AI and prompt injection are prohibited. Details of the review process (blind or not, number of reviewers) were in collapsed sections not captured: not confirmed. A post-publication policy and no-show policy exist (not read).
- Registration fee: not published (registration opens March 2027; the registration page returned 404).
- Fit: good (imaging, informatics, AI). Timing: about 12 weeks from today, compatible with final-model selection today.

## 3. Recommendation
1. IEEE EMBC 2027: only candidate with a complete, official, post-today schedule, IEEE society sponsorship and an IEEE template. Needs a 4+1 page paper and a named reviewer among the authors.
2. Backup, only if the authors want an India-based IEEE event: CONECCT 2027 (15 December 2026) after confirming a suitable track and page limit when the CFP opens. Do not rely on ICoNSIP until IEEE sponsorship is shown on the official site.
